## Notes

- The design was informed by the pilot results (results/pilot/). The methods must state this.
- Confidence intervals are percentile bootstrap intervals: lakes resampled with replacement, the same resamples for every rho within a sample.
- Area is the DNR acres attribute. Shoreline length is shoreline_m from src/filter_lakes.py.
